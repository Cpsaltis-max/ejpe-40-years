Article-orientation analysis

This analysis codes EJPE articles using the 1981 distinction between psychology applied to education and a psychology of education whose theoretical object is generated by educational situations, interactions and institutions.

The coding uses six article-orientation categories and treats context-sensitive educational psychology as an intermediate category rather than as the default expression of the founding project.
