Citation Lineages

These figures examine the intellectual sources cited by EJPE articles and ask how different cited traditions are taken up by different kinds of educational-psychology papers.

How to Read This Section

The left side of the main alluvial diagram classifies cited authors by their primary intellectual lineage. The right side classifies the EJPE articles that cite them. A flow therefore means: articles of this orientation draw on authors from this intellectual lineage.

Lineages to Article Orientations

This is the clearest genealogy figure. It shows whether EJPE papers coded as fundamental, context-sensitive, applied, theoretical/reflexive, methodological, or unclear draw on different cited intellectual traditions.

Cited intellectual lineages on the left; orientation of citing EJPE articles on the right. Hover over flows to see percentages within each cited lineage and raw article-author citation counts.

The strongest general pattern is that many traditions flow into context-sensitive educational psychology. The sociocultural/dialogic and interactionist constructivist lineages, however, retain a visibly stronger connection to papers coded as fundamental educational interaction.
