Headline Argument

EJPE began close to a founding social constructivist and socio-institutional project of psychology of education. Over four decades, this project persisted, but became one lineage within a broader international educational psychology ecology shaped by motivation, self-regulation, achievement, psychometrics, policy evidence, and methodological standardization.
