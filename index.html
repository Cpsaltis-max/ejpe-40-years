Headline Argument

EJPE is treated here as a sociogenetic object: a journal produced through people, institutions, theories, languages, and political conditions, and later a medium through which new intellectual identities were produced. Its founding social constructivist and socio-institutional project persisted over four decades, but became one lineage within a broader international educational psychology ecology.
