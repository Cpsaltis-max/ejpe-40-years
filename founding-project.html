The founding social constructivist and socio-institutional project

These analyses examine whether EJPE papers treat education merely as a setting for applying psychological constructs, or as a socio-institutional and interactional situation that helps constitute learning, knowledge, development, and subjectivity.

Anne-Nelly Perret-Clermont and other founding figures define the broader project; Gilly's writings are used as explicit textual anchors for that project.
